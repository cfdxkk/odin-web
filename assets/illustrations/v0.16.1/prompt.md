# Odin print footer — v0.16.1

Mode: built-in Imagegen, using the actual shipped 3D model as the identity reference. The generated landscape is screened into an exact six-unit amplitude-modulated lattice by `tools/build-footer-halftone.cjs` and exported as a lossless PNG. An editable SVG retains every circle. No random positions, density or speckle are used.

Identity reference: [real-model capture](./odin-model-reference.png). Composition reference: the user's Ironclad Labs footer screenshot. No previously generated spacecraft was used as an identity reference.

Final prompt:

Use case: identity-preserve / illustration-story. Create the final landscape artwork for a dark website footer, panoramic 3:1.
Image 1 is the authoritative, REAL 3D model of the Anvil Odin spacecraft. Preserve THIS spacecraft's exact silhouette and proportions: exceptionally long slender angular naval hull, straight long bow toward the right, central stepped bridge tower with the radar sphere and platforms, paired large cylindrical engines at the aft left, split lower hull and visible angular side structures. Copy the ship from Image 1 into the new scene; do not invent a different ship. Do not shorten, widen, make it round, add a giant tower, add rows of turrets, or change its naval architecture. Remove the interface button and every other UI element from Image 1.
Image 2 is ONLY the reference for the printed landscape treatment: layered mountain valleys, fine regularly aligned halftone dots, restrained tones and atmospheric depth. Ignore its text, company logo, green color, links and page UI.
Create a calm lower-half landscape of receding mountain ridges and a misty valley. Place the exact Odin from Image 1 in the middle distance among the mountains, about 34 percent of the panorama width, same side-view angle with bow right. Its full silhouette must stay recognizable and some lower foreground terrain should integrate it into the scene. The mountains enter at both bottom corners and recede through the center. The upper third is empty, uniform near-black #070b10 with the mountains dissolving gently into it.
Rendering style: an elegant monochrome newspaper / comic print, consistent fine circular halftone screen with dots on precisely aligned rows and columns. Ship and mountains share the SAME print treatment. Subdued warm light-gray ink on charcoal, with dot size changing to produce tonal areas. Connected ink in denser areas, small negative holes; no random stippling, no sparse glowing pixels, no grain or noise, no etched crosshatching. Calm layered valley shapes like Image 2, no huge dramatic bright snowy alpine peaks. No colors, no gradients on the sky, no stars, no engine flame, no typography, no watermark, no frame. The goal is faithful actual Odin plus the reference's fine printed mountain composition, adapted to a black website.
